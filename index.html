A model of the coffee world

Coffee Atlas is an exploratory prototype. All coffee figures are synthetic, plausible examples in one illustrative annual scenario. They are not current statistics, forecasts, farm quotations, or a complete global dataset.

  • Coverage: 34 producing countries. Summary totals and production shares refer only to these origins. Production is shown as tonnes of unroasted green coffee; one 60 kg bag equals 0.06 tonnes.
  • Bean filters: filters choose whole origins. Arabica-led means at least 80% Arabica; Robusta-led means at least 80% Robusta. Mixed systems means neither bean reaches 80%. All metrics and routes retain the whole origin’s production. The paired summary percentages are volume-weighted across the visible origins, and mint-to-amber country colors in Composition show each country’s Arabica / Robusta balance.
  • Map scales: country colors change with the layer. Production uses a square-root color scale from 0 to 3.9 million tonnes; cost uses a linear $1–$5 per kg blue scale. Composition blends amber (100% Robusta) to mint (100% Arabica). Flows colors origins by export intensity from 0–100%, with blue import-market highlights. Trade-line widths are proportional to export volume within the selected origin. Fixed scales are retained across filters so countries remain comparable. Exact values appear in tooltips, country details, and the sortable table.
  • Cost: illustrative farm production cost per kg of green coffee. It excludes trading margins, freight, import duties, roasting, and retail. The cost split is a modeled allocation, not an audited farm budget. Yield is tonnes per harvested hectare.
  • Flows: export volume equals production × export intensity. Four representative markets receive a simplified 100% allocation of those exports; real trade includes many more partners and re-exports. Curves show relationships, not actual shipping lanes. Import destinations are not equivalent to final consumption.
  • Geography: Natural Earth public-domain map geometry. Markers represent growing regions approximately. Boundaries are for orientation.

The app runs entirely in your browser, with no account or live data connection.
